Brinkcasters

Push them over the edge.

A knockback spell-brawler for phones. Two teams of casters duel on a crumbling island above lava, ice and the void: every hit makes your foe hotter and easier to throw, and the last one standing on the brink wins the round. Draft a new spell between rounds, empower it, and play 2v2, 1v1 or free-for-all.
