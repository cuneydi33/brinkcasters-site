Brinkcasters: Support

Write to viralexee@gmail.com and tell us your phone model and what happened. We answer within two days.

How to play

Move with the left stick, aim and cast with the spell buttons on the right. Hits raise your foe's heat: the hotter a caster, the further they fly. Knock enemies off the island; the island shrinks every round. Between rounds the Codex lets you draft a new spell or empower one you have.

Online play

Online matches are coming in a later update. For now every mode is played against computer opponents, with four difficulty levels.

My progress

Progress is saved on the phone. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Purchases

This version has no real-money purchases; everything is earned by playing.

See also the privacy policy.
